        06f

        The top down read, market by market

        Parallax runs three daily cuts of the same tape. The basket cut is breadth, how many thematic baskets in a market rose. The factor cut is a long short book per factor per market, so a factor sitting in Laggards means long minus short lost, not that the longs fell. The macro cut prices four macro axes and carries its own confidence flag. The fund holds none of these baskets, so this section sets the backdrop and does not name a trade. Weights here follow the listing venue, because that is how the telemetry baskets are built. Section 06c attributes the same lines to the company's home market instead, which is why China carries weight there and not here.

            10

            The weekly cycle

            One command, Saturday before class. python3 weekly_update.py sits beside this file. It refreshes the scores, applies the gates, ranks the replacements and rewrites the generated part of this page. The written reasoning in the reason column is a separate map the script never touches, so the numbers update without losing the argument.

              Two habits make the difference. Run it before deciding anything, because the whole reason three momentum names broke their trigger in August is that the screen ran on a score file five days old. And when a slot cannot be filled without breaking a gate, leave the weight in the ACWI proxy rather than relaxing the gate, because a gate relaxed once stops being a rule.
