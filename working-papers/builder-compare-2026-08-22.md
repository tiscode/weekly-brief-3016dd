# What the universe builder would hold, against what we hold

22 August 2026

Parallax's universe builder was asked to construct each sleeve from scratch, at the confirmed slot counts of nine momentum and six value, globally diversified. Seven passes were run, one per region per factor. The builder did the ranking. Only two constraints were imposed on top: a tradeable currency, because the simulator cannot fill mainland A-shares, Korea or Taiwan whatever the score says, and at most two names per market and two per sector, which is the diversification instruction.

**Our own sleeve gates were deliberately not applied.** Imposing them would have reproduced our book with extra steps. The value of the exercise is that the two constructions are independent.

## Six things this says

**1. Zero overlap.** Not one name in common, fifteen against sixteen. Two processes on the same underlying data produce entirely disjoint books. That is worth knowing before anyone claims either construction is objective.

**2. Our momentum sleeve is not global, and the builder's is.** Nine United States names in one currency against six markets and six currencies. The project guide's own checklist asks that the process be deliberately global rather than a US portfolio with a global label. On the momentum sleeve we fail that test outright and the builder does not. This is the single strongest argument in the builder's favour and it is not a scoring argument at all.

**3. The builder wins the factor it sorted on and loses the blend.** Its momentum sleeve averages 9.6 on momentum against our 8.3, which is what sorting on one column does. But its average total is 8.8 against our 9.6 and its average timing is 4.4 against our 6.2. Ranking on a single factor buys that factor and pays for it everywhere else.

**4. Most of the builder's names would fail our own rules.** Only **3 of 9** momentum names clear a total of 9 with timing at 5 or better. Only **1 of 6** value names has value, quality and momentum all at 8. So the disagreement is not marginal, it is structural: our gates demand the blend plus a timing confirmation, and the builder is answering a different question.

**5. Higher upside, concentrated in a few extremes.** The builder's momentum sleeve averages +23.4% against our +14.4%, and four names carry it: Micron +57%, Furukawa Electric +62%, Technoprobe +50% and China Unicom +51%. Those are high-dispersion semiconductor and telecom names where the analyst range is enormous. Strip them and the advantage largely goes. Our book is tighter and duller.

**6. The builder's value sleeve is actually cheap, and ours has never been measured.** Median PE 13.8 and price to book 0.58 against a momentum sleeve at 22.9 and 2.52. Our value sleeve is selected on a vendor value score and we have never looked at a multiple on it. Calling a sleeve value without checking what it costs is a gap a marker could reasonably press on, and the builder exposes it for free.

## Two data notes

Kazatomprom's target endpoint quotes a current price of 29.40 against the builder's 70.20, which is a unit or listing mismatch rather than a 213% upside. Its upside is excluded from every average above.

China Resources Power appeared in the builder's value pass with a total of 9.6 and `pick_eligible: false`. That is the line that never filled in our own book, which suggests the eligibility flag is worth reading before ordering rather than after.

## What to do with this

The honest read is that neither book should replace the other. The builder's contribution is the geography: it demonstrates that a globally spread momentum sleeve is available and that ours is a US sleeve by accident rather than by choice. Its contribution is not stock selection, because two thirds of its names cannot clear the rules the group wrote and has been applying consistently.

Two concrete actions follow. Add a market or currency cap to the momentum screen so the sleeve cannot end up nine-for-nine in one country again. And add a valuation sanity check to the value sleeve, because a sleeve called value that has never been tested against a multiple is a presentation risk.
