## Three qualifications on executing the list

**Sector concentration.** The redundancy check on the target book returns issues. Healthcare 13.46
percent, Financials 8.08, with industry overlap flagged twice, on managed healthcare and on oil and
gas refining. Yesterday's sector read already put Health Care 7.76 points overweight against ACWI
in a sector Parallax ranks 11 of 11 in the United States. Adding CVS, 6160.HK and OMCL.O keeps that
overweight where it is.

**Country concentration.** Hong Kong listings at 21.54 percent are 31 percent of the single name
sleeve. That is inside any reasonable limit but it is the largest single venue bet the fund would
be taking, and it sits in the one venue with no country call behind it.

**The gate that produced the sells.** Six of seven sells are tactical median failures. Measured on
our own panel this week, tactical carries no directional information at the single name level: over
50 weekly cross sections the names scoring 9 or 10 returned 0.06 points more than the panel at one
week and 0.34 points less at 13 weeks, while the names scoring 0 to 3 beat them at every horizon.
The four week median rule still tested well as a portfolio construction, plus 0.95 percent over 13
weeks with 84 percent of weeks won, so the rule is not being contradicted at the level it was built
for. But selling FDX at a total of 9.9 because one unstable band printed low deserves to be a
decision the meeting takes, not one the script takes.
