## What this mark does not carry

- The four non-USD lines are held at the rate implied by the 19 August book. A real move in the Canadian dollar, the Hong Kong dollar or the Australian dollar is not in these numbers. Drop an fx.json beside this script, keyed by mark date, and it is used instead.
- The target book adds international names that need rates this file does not have: CHF for 1 line, EUR for 2 lines, GBP for 1 line, HKD for 6 lines, JPY for 1 line, SEK for 1 line. They are not marked here because they are not bought.
- Fills are still unconfirmed. Entry prices are the 2026-08-18 closes, which is the assumption the whole table rests on. The StockTrak equity figure is the only number that settles it, and it is recorded here whenever it is passed in.
