# The vendor's own analytics, 2026-08-23

Four Parallax endpoints that had never been run against this book. The book is the account's own on 2026-08-21: 19 lines, weights renormalised to exclude the 8.20% cash, benchmark ACWI, base currency US dollars, 365 calendar days to the day before the pull. Every figure below is the vendor's, quoted as returned.

## Read the return numbers as hindsight, not as performance

The analyse endpoint takes today's weights and walks them back a year. Today's weights were chosen on a momentum band measured this month, so the walk-back is a list of names already known to have risen. It shows: all 19 of 19 lines are positive over the year, the weakest of them +11.5%, the average +43.7%, against a benchmark of +21.6%. No live portfolio has that hit rate.

The split is clean, and it is not a matter of degree:

- **Risk numbers hold.** Tracking error, beta, correlation, R-squared, the volatility level, the drawdown shape and every concentration measure are properties of the weights and of how the nineteen lines move together. Selecting winners does not make two airlines stop correlating at 0.9.
- **Return numbers do not hold.** Total return, alpha, the information ratio, both capture ratios, Sharpe and Sortino all divide or subtract a return that was chosen with the answer in hand. None of them belongs on a slide as performance.

Realised performance has exactly one source: the account. It is in `weeks-*.md` and on the chart at the top of the brief, and it covers the two weeks the fund has actually been running.

What does survive on every line is the return set. Return on equity, return on assets, return on invested capital and the margins divide one accounting figure by another in the same currency, so PetroChina at 10.15% and Rio Tinto at 16.97% stand.

Yesterday's paper put the builder's own value book at a median price to earnings of 13.8 and a price to book of 0.58 and treated that as the standard ours had never been measured against. Of its 6 names, 0 trade in United States dollars: the currencies are GBP, HKD, JPY, SEK. The correction matters because the direction was flattering. What the builder run does still establish stands untouched, because it never depended on a ratio: nine momentum slots came back as six markets in six currencies against our nine names in one.

## Why a value band of 10 sits on a P/E of 34

The vendor's own definition settles it. The value band measures "P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, P/S ratios, and dividend yield relative to peers". Two things follow, and both need saying out loud in the write-up:

1. It is **relative to peers**, so a band of 10 says cheap against its own industry, not cheap. A sleeve selected on the band is a sleeve of relative winners inside whatever industries the screen happened to reach.
2. It blends **five multiples**, of which earnings is one. A name on a depressed profit can carry a high band on price to book and price to sales while its P/E reads like a growth stock.

The vendor's own peer tables show both effects directly:

## The citation behind each band, for the methodology slide

Quoted from the vendor rather than paraphrased, because the integrity rules ask for every number to be explainable and these are the sources.

| Band | Measures | Academic basis |
|---|---|---|
| Value Score | P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, P/S ratios, and dividend yield relative to peers | Fama-French (1992), Graham & Dodd (1934) |
| Quality Score | ROE, ROA, profit margins, balance sheet strength, earnings stability, forensic accounting flags | Asness, Frazzini & Pedersen (2019), Piotroski F-Score (2000) |
| Momentum Score | 6/12-month price returns, earnings revisions, revenue growth, relative strength | Jegadeesh & Titman (1993) |
| Defensive Score | Historical volatility, beta, earnings stability, dividend quality, recession resistance | Baker, Bradley & Wurgler (2011), minimum variance theory |
| Tactical Score | Institutional flows, insider trading, technical signals, event-driven catalysts | Kyle Model (1985), Campbell, Grossman & Wang (1993) |

The blended total is not a fixed weighted average. An ML model trained on macro conditions sets factor weights (10-40% each). In growth regimes, momentum and quality get more weight; in defensive regimes, defensive and value dominate. Basis: Asness et al. (2017), Arnott et al. (2019). The recommendation bands on the total are STRONG BUY at 8.5-10, BUY at 6.5-8.4, HOLD at 3.5-6.4, SELL at 1.5-3.4, STRONG SELL at 0-1.4.

Two consequences for the write-up. The gate at a total of 9.0 is stricter than the vendor's own STRONG BUY, which begins at 8.5. And because the weights move with the macro regime, a total that falls without any band falling is the regime being reweighted, not the company changing, which is worth saying before somebody asks.

Documentation: [Value Score](https://docs.chicago.global/methodology/factors#value), [Quality Score](https://docs.chicago.global/methodology/factors#quality), [Momentum Score](https://docs.chicago.global/methodology/factors#momentum), [Defensive Score](https://docs.chicago.global/methodology/factors#defensive), [Tactical Score](https://docs.chicago.global/methodology/factors#tactical), [Overall Score](https://docs.chicago.global/methodology/scoring).

## The currency cap now in the screen

`weekly_update.py` gains `CCY_CAP = 3`, names per trading currency per sleeve, sitting beside the existing sector cap of 3. The case for it is the market Herfindahl of 0.814 above, and the check that it is achievable is the eligible pool on the 2026-08-21 snapshot: 49 names clear the total and tactical floors, spread USD 18, HKD 16, CAD 4, EUR 3, GBP 3, JPY 2, SEK 1, AUD 1, CHF 1. Nine momentum slots at three per currency therefore need three currencies and can reach nine.

Three design choices worth recording. The cap counts **currency** rather than the market label, matching the existing candidate filter and for the same reason: the vendor files PetroChina under China while the line the fund holds settles in Hong Kong dollars. The cap is **preferential**, applied on a first pass and then lifted to fill any slot still empty, because a vacancy costs more than a fourth name in one currency; names taken on the relaxed pass are tagged. And it applies **per sleeve**, so the value sleeve's own spread is judged on its own.

## What is still not being used

- `get_technical_analysis` returns support, resistance, RSI and MACD. The momentum sell rule is a hand-built 8% band off the 252-session high; a real support level would be a better trigger and is already computed.
- `get_financial_analysis` runs a Palepu-framework read per name, asynchronously. Worth one pull on the two review lines the group is closest to selling.
- `rolling_metrics` and `monthly_returns` inside the analyse endpoint were left out of the field subset to keep the response readable. Add them the week the write-up needs a rolling beta chart.
- `check_portfolio_redundancy` returns "well-diversified" on a book holding two airlines at 0.910 and 90% of its weight in one country. It stays unused. The correlation matrix in `stats-*.md` found what it missed.
